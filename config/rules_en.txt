HOUSE RULES — SHORT-TERM RENTAL

1. Couples and marital status
In accordance with Moroccan law, a couple in which at least one person is a Moroccan national must present a marriage certificate. Unmarried couples that include a Moroccan national are not allowed. The host may ask for any supporting document and refuse access to the property.

2. Alcohol, drugs and illegal substances
Bringing, keeping or consuming alcohol, drugs or any illegal substance is strictly forbidden in the property and its common areas.

3. Prostitution and illegal activities
Any form of prostitution, soliciting or procuring, and any illegal activity (trafficking, handling stolen goods, illegal gambling, etc.) is strictly forbidden and will be reported to the authorities.

4. Parties, gatherings and visitors
Parties, gatherings and events are forbidden. No undeclared visitor is allowed in the property, day or night.

5. Occupancy
Only the people declared at registration may stay in the property. Subletting, lending the property, or hosting additional people without the host's written consent is forbidden.

6. Check-in and check-out times
The guest must respect the check-in and check-out times set by the host and leave the property at the agreed time. The guest has no right to rent, sublet or make the property available to other people during the stay.

7. Quiet hours
Quiet must be respected between 10:00 PM and 8:00 AM. Loud music, shouting and any excessive noise are forbidden at all times.

8. Smoking, fire and safety
Smoking indoors is forbidden (cigarettes, shisha, e-cigarettes). Candles, fireworks, barbecues and open flames are forbidden. Weapons and dangerous items are forbidden. Close doors and windows when you go out and report any incident immediately.

9. Care of the property
Any damage, theft or missing equipment will be charged to the guest. Changing the layout or installations is forbidden. Pets are not allowed without the host's prior consent.

10. Commercial use, photos and recordings
Filming, commercial photo shoots and any professional use of the property are forbidden without written consent. Installing cameras or any recording device is forbidden.

11. General conduct
Guests agree to respect the property, its equipment, the host and the neighbours, as well as the laws and customs of the Kingdom of Morocco. Any threatening or violent behaviour, or behaviour that endangers people or the property, is forbidden.

12. Sanctions
Any breach of these rules ends the stay immediately without refund, may lead to the deposit being kept, and may be reported to the authorities and lead to civil or criminal proceedings.

13. Personal data
The information collected is used only for the legal registration of guests with the authorities and for managing the rental.
