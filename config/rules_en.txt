SEASONAL RENTAL CONTRACT — HOUSE RULES AND CONDITIONS

1. Contract holder and accompanying people
The person who rented the property is responsible for everyone who accompanies them in the property and makes them follow the house rules. The guest certifies that the information provided is accurate.

2. Property capacity
Respect the maximum capacity allowed for the property.

3. Visitors
Do not receive any undeclared visitor or guest without the host's prior written consent.

4. Neighbours, noise and parties
Respect the neighbours, avoid any noise between 10:00 PM and 9:00 AM, and do not hold any party or event in the property.

5. Care of the property
Keep the property in good condition, as a careful person would, and report any damage or incident to the host immediately.

6. Check-in and check-out times
Respect the check-in and check-out times set by the host, and leave the property at the agreed time.

7. Keys and equipment
Return the keys and equipment provided at the end of the stay.

8. No renting or subletting
Do not rent or sublet the property, and do not lend it or make it available to other people during the stay.

9. Couples
Any couple that includes a Moroccan national must present a marriage certificate; otherwise it is not allowed.

10. Alcohol, drugs and illegal activities
Alcohol, drugs, illegal substances, prostitution and any illegal activity are strictly forbidden and will be reported to the authorities.

11. Moroccan law
Respect the laws and regulations in force in the Kingdom of Morocco.

12. Personal data
The host keeps a copy or a photograph of the guest's identity document (national ID or passport), only to register the stay and meet the legal obligation to declare guests.

13. Responsibility and termination
The guest is responsible for damage, loss and theft occurring during the stay, and for the actions of their companions and visitors. If the rules are breached, the host may terminate the contract immediately without prior notice, and the guest must then leave the property at once.
